# PokeRecomp

PokeRecomp is a native reimplementation of Pokémon Gold, Silver and Crystal,
built from scratch in Godot 4 and GDScript. It is not an emulator: bring your
own verified cartridge dump and the game decodes it locally.

The playable alpha supports Gold, Silver and Crystal across Windows, macOS,
Linux, Android, iPhone and iPad, Nintendo Switch homebrew and ARM devices. It
also supports keyboards, gamepads and touch controls.

## Highlights

- Open-source Godot 4 engine under the MIT licence
- Native builds for eight platforms
- Built-in mod support for content, art and renderer replacements
