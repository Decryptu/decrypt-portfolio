# PokeLDN

PokeLDN connects a computer to retail Pokémon games over the Switch's local
wireless protocol. My ESP32 firmware handles the radio over USB, bringing the
desktop app to macOS, Windows and Linux. Nothing is installed on the Switch
or Switch 2.

![PokeLDN desktop app offering a shiny Chansey for a FireRed trade](/images/pokeldn-app.webp)

I designed and built the full app, including its UI and UX, the ESP32 firmware
and the game protocol implementations. The interface flashes the board,
builds legal Pokémon, queues trades and shows incoming Pokémon live.

Trading works across seven game families, from FireRed and LeafGreen to
Legends Z-A, with Mystery Gift and link battles in supported games.
